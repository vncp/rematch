A harness has eight parts an improver can edit. rematch maps each to one omp component and gives each its own activation rule: skill, rule and fact text, data-only hooks (`block`, `stuck`, `truncate`, `retrieve`, `check`), and read-only subagents activate automatically as scoped provisional trials (listed under `applied_automatically` with an undo command); personal `AGENTS.md` instructions, write-capable subagents, and scripts wait in the persistent action queue for your approval (unless covered by a standing `policy auto` rule; scripts include observed sandbox dry-run writes/network/exit); anything beyond your machine waits for a branch or PR.

Who approves what, and how team and fork changes leave your machine: [the evidence boundary](/rematch/concepts/evidence-boundary/#scope-and-who-merges).

Harness² edits eight surfaces (research D3). Each maps to one omp component; activation follows SPEC §"Scope and who merges" as amended by D29–D32: skill, rule and fact text, data-only hooks (`block`, `stuck`, `truncate`, `retrieve`, `check`), and read-only subagents (`tools:` frontmatter a subset of `library::READ_ONLY_AGENT_TOOLS`) activate automatically as scoped `provisional` trials and are listed under `applied_automatically` with one undo command (`rematch library rollback <name>` or `rematch library disable <id>`; `rematch off` withdraws all); personal instructions, write-capable subagents, and scripts (unless covered by a standing `policy.json` `auto` rule; scripts include observed sandbox dry-run writes/network/exit on the review item) wait in the persistent action queue (§14 "Actions and approvals") for an approval bound to the reviewed snapshot; team and fork changes leave only as a branch/PR the binary never merges.
